# Project progress

Updated: 11 November 2026.

The six-stage analytical study is complete. The [portfolio case study](https://jeks042.github.io/ecommerce-funnel-revenue.html), [illustrated report](commercial_report.md) and [decision memo](decision_memo.md) support an investigation of product view to cart across devices. Verify recording and study shopper tasks before deciding whether to test a clearer cart action. The study does not establish abandonment, a cause or recoverable sales.

The December primary sequence remains 27,289 product-view visits, 7,874 later carts, 2,819 later checkouts and 1,437 later qualified completions. Separately, 1,976 of 131,418 eligible visits contain a qualified purchase. Ninety-two visits contain unresolved purchases. Total revenue, AOV and revenue per visit remain unavailable.

Mobile's overall recorded progression is slightly higher than desktop's; the date-adjusted difference is +0.28 percentage points. Checkout-only progression points the other way and is date-sensitive. First-user context remains descriptive, with 35,754 eligible visits lacking interpretable context. These associations do not justify a mobile-only redesign or advertising allocation.

## Final reproduction

The clean milestone 5 checkout passed its 57 tests, regenerated both summaries without tracked changes and reproduced all eight figure files byte for byte on Windows. Thirteen fresh read-only warehouse queries reconciled all eight model fingerprints, 995 source checks and 4,682 funnel/reverse checks. Their review and the excluded cached attempt remain in the [release receipt](evidence/release-receipt-20261111.json).

## What a product team would need next

The [experiment proposal](../docs/experiment_proposal.md) uses stable visitor IDs and seven-day outcomes. Its nine power illustrations use assumed visitor baselines. Production baseline, business target, safety margins, prototype research, identity reliability and duration remain launch requirements. No trial has begun; no uplift or return has been measured.

The eight Sandbox tables expire on 5 January 2027. Reviewed aggregates and executable SQL remain in GitHub. Configuration, source identifiers, full job references and private career material stay outside the public release.
